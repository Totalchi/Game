# WARDBOUND — World Bible & Art Direction

> Purpose: give the game a soul, the artists a north star, and the content team years of runway.
> The world exists to justify and amplify the mechanic — not decorate it.

## 1. The premise: the Long Dusk

The world is called **Vael**. Once it was lit and ruled by six living elemental powers — the
**Primarchs** of Ember, Tide, Storm, Stone, Bloom, and Frost. They were not gods exactly; they were
the *forces themselves*, awake and whole.

Then came the **Sundering** — a wound torn at the center of the world by something that should not
have a name (the seventh thing; the **Hollow**). The six Primarchs were shattered. They did not
vanish cleanly. Their deaths *echoed*, and the echoes — fragments of dead elemental will, hungry and
half-mad — became the **Wraiths** that now roam Vael.

Since the Sundering the world has been dimming. No true day, no true night: only the **Long Dusk**,
a permanent amber-gray twilight. And the world's heart is failing — you can feel it beat, slow and
fading, once every **600 milliseconds**.

## 2. The heartbeat (why the tick is canon)

This is the keystone idea that ties mechanic, audio, art, and story into one knot:

> **The tick is the dying heartbeat of the world, and Wardens flick in time with the apocalypse.**

- The 600ms pulse is **The Knell** — the world's failing heartbeat. Everyone in Vael can feel it.
- **Wardens** are those who learned to *move on the beat* — to raise an elemental Ward on the exact
  Knell that a blow lands. Warding is, literally, syncing your soul to the world's heart for one beat.
- This means our core UI element (the tick pulse) is **diegetic**: it's not a metronome we bolted on,
  it's the heartbeat of Vael made visible. Audio, VFX, and lore all reinforce the same rhythm.
- **Endgame stakes:** the antagonist seeks to *silence the Knell* — to stop the heart for good. The
  final act literally destabilizes the tick (see §6), making the mechanic itself the climax.

## 3. The Wardens (the player fantasy)

- An **order in decline**, scattered survivors who kept the old discipline of the Ward.
- A Warden doesn't command Wraiths by force — they **bind** them by proving mastery of the same
  rhythm the Wraith died to (the Binding Rite). It's a relationship of respect/dominance, not capture.
- Player identity: a hooded figure with a **Ward-sigil** (a six-spoked wheel that lights up per
  element as you flick). Prime cosmetic surface (sigils, cloaks, the light of your Wards).

## 4. The six domains (regions = elements = art bibles = content)

Each shattered Primarch left a **domain** twisted by its death. These are our regions, art bibles,
Wraith habitats, and the spine of the Story/Hunt content.

| Domain | Element | Mood & art notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Cinderwaste** | Ember | Ash deserts under a smoke-red dusk; glass-fused dunes, guttering ember-light, the bones of a burned city. |
| **The Drowned Reach** | Tide | Endless tidal flats and sunken halls; lantern-light under black water, drowned bells, weeping fog. |
| **The Howling Span** | Storm | Shattered sky-bridges between floating stone; shrieking wind, perpetual lightning, vertigo and verticality. |
| **The Cairnlands** | Stone | Grave-moors of standing stones and barrows; heavy, still, ancient; the quietest, most oppressive zone. |
| **The Rotwood** | Bloom | A vast fungal forest feeding on the dead; bioluminescent decay, beautiful and sickly, overgrowth swallowing ruins. |
| **The Rime** | Frost | A frozen waste of black ice; the cold death; silence, white-blue gloom, things frozen mid-scream. |
| **The Hollow** ★ | Void | The wound at the center where the Sundering began; non-Euclidean, dim, *wrong*. Endgame. Where the Knell is loudest and weakest at once. |

## 5. Visual & audio direction

**Visual:**
- **Palette:** desaturated dusk base (amber-grays) so the **element colors pop** as pure signal. Each
  element owns a hue *and a shape language* (colorblind-safe, gameplay-legible):
  Ember = jagged orange · Tide = flowing cyan · Storm = forked violet · Stone = blocky amber-brown ·
  Bloom = spreading green · Frost = crystalline white-blue · Hollow = absence/black with a thin rim-light.
- **Wraiths:** dead-elemental spirits with an eldritch edge — not cute mascots, not gore. Think
  "beautiful ghost made of dying fire/water/etc." Silhouette-first design (readable at small size and
  in spectator HUD).
- **Combat clarity over spectacle:** the screen must always read at a glance — telegraph element,
  landing beat, your energy, your Resolve. Juice serves clarity, never buries it.

**Audio:**
- **The Knell** is the spine of the mix — a low, resonant pulse on every tick. Music is *built around*
  it (BPM math: 600ms = 100 BPM; everything composes to the heartbeat).
- A flick has a satisfying, element-tuned "chime"; **Perfect** has a distinct, addictive "snap" the
  whole game trains you to crave. Different per element so skilled players play partly *by ear*.

## 6. Story spine (the single-player arc)

A lightweight but resonant frame around the gameplay ramp:

1. **Awakening.** You're a late-born Warden who can feel the Knell unusually clearly. Learn to flick. Bind your first Wraith.
2. **The Six Domains.** Travel the shattered domains, binding Wraiths, facing a **boss echo** of each dead Primarch (each a signature-mechanic skill-check that teaches a new flick layer).
3. **The Hollowed.** A fallen Warden, **Mourne**, has gone into the Hollow believing the only mercy is to *end the suffering world* — to **silence the Knell**. As you near them, the tick begins to **stutter and shift** (the world's heart skipping), turning the mechanic itself unstable.
4. **The Last Beat.** Final confrontation in the Hollow where the tick destabilizes (variable cadence, decaying Wards via the **Unmaking** curse). You must flick a *failing* heartbeat back into rhythm — the ultimate expression of everything you've learned.

(Open-ended on purpose for seasons: the Knell stabilized but not healed → new Wraiths, new domains, the question of whether Vael *should* be saved.)

## 7. Naming bible (keep it consistent + original)

- **World:** Vael · **The Long Dusk** · **The Sundering** · **The Knell** (the tick/heartbeat).
- **Forces:** the **Primarchs** (dead elemental gods) → their echoes are **Wraiths**.
- **Player:** **Warden** · their power is **Warding** · the shield is a **Ward** · the catch ritual is the **Binding Rite**.
- **Resources:** **Aether** (energy) · **Resolve** (counter-meter) · **Vigor** (HP).
- **Elements:** Ember, Tide, Storm, Stone, Bloom, Frost, **Hollow** (★ void).
- **Antagonist:** **Mourne, the Hollowed.**

> Consistency rule: when in doubt, use this bible. New names should sound like they belong to a tired,
> mythic, dying-but-beautiful world. Avoid anything that reads like OSRS or Pokémon terminology.
