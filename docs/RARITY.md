# WARDBOUND — Rarity & The Chase

> The dream of a collector game is *"I have to catch that one."* This is how WARDBOUND builds
> obsession — ethically, with **skill and dedication as the currency, never your wallet.**

## 1. Core principle

**Rarity is earned, not bought.** You can buy *cosmetics*; you can **never** buy a rare Wraith or pay
to make a Binding easier. The rarest creatures are trophies that say *"I'm good"* and *"I was there"* —
which is exactly what makes them worth chasing and worth bragging about. (Ties to MONETIZATION.md.)

## 2. The rarity tiers

| Tier | Name | Where/how | Binding Rite | Vibe |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ⚪ **Common** | **Ashen** | Everywhere in the domains | Short, forgiving | The roster backbone; first friends. |
| 🟢 **Uncommon** | **Touched** | Common, slightly deeper zones | Moderate | Solid team-builders. |
| 🔵 **Rare** | **Wraithtouched** | Specific zones/conditions | Hard, longer sequence | The meta picks; real catches. |
| 🟣 **Very Rare** | **Revenants** | Rare conditional spawns (see §4) | Brutal, multi-phase Rite | Apex Wraiths. A genuine accomplishment. |
| 🟠 **Extremely Rare** | **Primarch Echoes** | World-event / story-gated, one-at-a-time | Boss-tier Rite + skill gate | The death-echoes of the dead gods. Mythic. |
| 🕳 **Mythic / Forbidden** | **Hollowborn** | The Hollow; hardest content in the game | The hardest Rite in WARDBOUND | Rule-breaking void Wraiths (e.g. Voidmoth Mourne). The "did you master it?" trophy. |

> **Plus a cross-cutting prestige variant (our "shiny"):** see §5 — **Aberrants**.

## 3. What makes a rare Wraith *feel* rare (the dopamine design)

- **Visual aura.** Rarer Wraiths emit a tier-coded aura/particle signature visible the instant they
  appear — the heart-skip "oh my god it's a Revenant" moment. Streamer gold.
- **A distinct spawn cue.** The Knell changes, the screen dims, the music shifts. The world *announces*
  something rare without a popup. (Diegetic, dramatic, clip-able.)
- **A real fight to bind.** Higher tiers = longer, nastier **Binding Rites** with feints, splits,
  stacks, and Curses. You earn it with your hands.
- **Loss is possible.** Fail a high-tier Rite and it can flee (with a cooldown before it can be found
  again). Stakes make the win matter. (Tunable; never punishing enough to feel cheap.)
- **Bragging surface.** Bound rares display in your Sanctuary's **Archive** and on your public Warden
  profile with a "bound on [date], odds, no-hit?" stamp. Collection as identity.

## 4. How the rarest spawn (skill + time + world state, never money)

The chase is driven by **world conditions**, so the world feels alive and players keep showing up:

- **Knell Stutters** — periodic world events where the heartbeat falters; rare/very-rare spawns surge.
  (Drives daily/weekly logins — see SEASONS.md.)
- **Eclipses & Domain Storms** — timed atmospheric events per domain that unlock conditional spawns.
- **Conditional triggers** — some Revenants appear only after: a perfect-flick streak, a no-hit Trial
  clear, binding the rest of an element's line, or reaching a Sanctuary milestone. **Mastery unlocks
  the dream.**
- **Primarch Echoes** are **story- and achievement-gated** — e.g. the Ember Echo appears only after you
  ease the Ember Primarch's death in Act II *and* clear its skill trial. One per world-state; everyone
  gets a fair, earned shot.
- **Hollowborn** live in the endgame Hollow content — the hardest Rites in the game. Pure skill gates.

## 5. Aberrants — the prestige variant ("shiny" done right)

- **What:** any Wraith can, very rarely, appear **Aberrant** — Hollow-corruption gives it a unique
  recolor/altered design, special VFX, and a marked profile badge. Cosmetic only — **no stat
  advantage** (skill purity holds).
- **Why it's perfect for us:** infinite long-tail chase, immense collector prestige, zero balance
  impact, and a natural **cosmetic economy** anchor (players adore showing off Aberrants).
- **Ethics:** Aberrants are *found through play*, not sold. We may sell *unrelated cosmetic skins*, but
  the Aberrant chase itself stays an earned flex. (If we ever offer paid cosmetic recolors, they're
  clearly distinct from earned Aberrants — no muddying the prestige.)

## 6. Collection meta-rewards (the completionist hook)

- **Domain Codex:** complete an element's line → Sanctuary perk + a cosmetic Ward-FX for that element.
- **The Full Bestiary:** long-horizon goal with a marquee cosmetic (a unique Warden cloak / sigil).
- **Aberrant Hall:** a Sanctuary wing that lights up per Aberrant bound — the ultimate flex room.
- **Seasonal Wraiths:** some Wraiths/Aberrants are season-limited (FOMO done gently — they rotate back,
  never paywalled), giving each season a fresh chase. (See SEASONS.md.)

## 7. Anti-frustration guardrails (so the chase stays fun, not grindy-toxic)

- **Bad luck protection:** soft pity on world-event spawns so dedicated hunters always trend toward a
  result. Time and skill *always* pay off eventually.
- **No energy/stamina gates** on hunting. Play as much as you want; we never sell "more attempts."
- **Skill can beat odds:** a great Warden binding a low-odds rare on a perfect Rite is a celebrated,
  intended outcome — skill is a legitimate path to luck.
- **Transparency:** spawn conditions are discoverable in-game (the Codex hints at them). Mystery, not
  obfuscation.

## 8. Why this drives a worldwide hit

- **Endless chase = endless retention** without pay-to-win resentment.
- **Rare-bind moments are the most shareable content** a collector game produces — free viral marketing.
- **Skill-gated rares** make the hardcore proud and the community aspirational — the engine of a lasting
  competitive + collecting culture.
