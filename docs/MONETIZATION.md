# WARDBOUND — Monetization & Business

> Model: **Free-to-play + cosmetics** (your choice — and the most ethical, highest-ceiling option
> that fits a skill game). No pay-to-win, ever: in a timing/skill game, selling power destroys the
> thing people came for. We sell *expression and identity*, not advantage.

## Guiding principle

**Skill must be the only thing that wins battles.** Everything purchasable is cosmetic or
convenience-that-doesn't-affect-balance. This protects the competitive integrity that makes the
game streamable and respected — which *is* the marketing engine.

## Revenue streams

### 1. Cosmetics (primary, long-term)
Cosmetics are ~42% of in-game purchase revenue across multiplayer titles — and they're the only
ethical lever for a skill game.

- **Wraith skins** — alternate art/forms (e.g. an Ascended "Hollow" variant of a starter).
- **Ward FX** — the visual/audio flourish when you flick a perfect Ward. *Highly* desirable because
  it's the most-repeated, most-watched action in the game. Prime cosmetic real estate.
- **Warden identity** — avatars, banners, victory poses, emotes, battle-arena themes.
- **Trails / hit sparks / tick-pulse skins** — small, cheap-to-make, collectible.

### 2. Battle Pass / "Warden's Pact" (seasonal)
Free track + premium track of cosmetic rewards. Drives retention and predictable revenue. Earnable
premium currency on the free track (goodwill + conversion).

### 3. Rewarded ads (web, optional & non-coercive)
On web portals, opt-in rewarded video for cosmetic currency / extra daily run. Never gates skill or
progress. This is the baseline revenue while the cosmetic economy matures.

### 4. Portal ad revshare (discovery + baseline income)
Publishing on web portals (Poki, CrazyGames, etc.) provides both **distribution** and **ad
revenue share**. Realistic early expectations for a first web game: **~$500–$3,000/month**, scaling
toward **~$1M/year** for established studios with a hit. We treat portals as a discovery + baseline
layer feeding players to our own site where cosmetics convert.

### 5. Later: premium platforms
A Steam release (premium "Deluxe" edition with cosmetic bundle, or F2P with cosmetics) and mobile
stores extend reach once the web game proves the loop.

## Distribution strategy

1. **Own site (hero channel):** the canonical WARDBOUND.* domain, with accounts + cosmetics.
   We keep ~100% of revenue from players who arrive here directly.
2. **Web portals (discovery):** simultaneously publish to Poki/CrazyGames for the traffic firehose;
   take ad revshare and funnel interested players to accounts.
3. **Streamers/creators (viral loop):** the skill ceiling + clip-ability is the cheapest marketing
   we have. Build in: instant replays, one-click clip export, spectacular perfect-flick FX,
   leaderboards, shareable Daily Ward seeds.
4. **Later:** Steam + mobile.

## Why this monetization fits THIS game

- A **skill game** has a passionate, retention-heavy core audience that *wants* to express identity
  → cosmetics convert well without resentment.
- The most-repeated action (the flick) is the perfect cosmetic surface — every player sees everyone
  else's Ward FX constantly. Built-in demand.
- No pay-to-win keeps the **competitive/streaming integrity** that drives free growth.

## Market context (why the bet is reasonable)

- Creature-collector / RPG demand is large and durable (RPGs ≈ 40% of the gacha market; gacha
  trending toward ~$45B by 2033).
- Rhythm/skill games are a growing, streamable niche ($2.5B → ~$7B by 2033).
- F2P is the dominant model on the channels we're targeting (≈96% of mobile downloads, ≈99% of
  Google Play revenue) — players expect free entry; we meet them there and monetize identity.

## Legal & IP (so this is actually sellable)

- **Game mechanics are not copyrightable** (ideas/rules/systems). Tick-flicking, type matchups, and
  collection are fair to reimplement. ✅
- **Expression IS protected**: art, character designs, names, sounds, specific UI. We build **100%
  original** assets, names, world, and code. ✅
- **Concrete rules we follow:**
  - No OSRS/Pokémon assets, names, sounds, creatures, or UI — ever.
  - Original element names (Ember/Tide/Storm/Stone/Bloom/Frost/Hollow), original creature
    ("Wraith") designs, original "Ward" naming.
  - Keep a clean-room mindset: inspired by the *feel*, never copying the *expression*.
- This is the Palworld/Stardew lesson: take the genre's mechanics, bring your own world.

## North-star metrics (what we'll watch)

- **D1 / D7 retention** (skill games live on "I'm getting better").
- **Tutorial→first-bind conversion** (the 3-minute ownership hook).
- **Free→payer conversion** and **ARPDAU** once cosmetics ship.
- **Clips/shares per 1k sessions** (our viral coefficient).
