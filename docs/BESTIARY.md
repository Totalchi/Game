# WARDBOUND — Bestiary (Launch Roster Concepts)

> A starting cast that proves the design: every signature ability **interacts with the flick loop**,
> not just stats. ~24–30 Wraiths ship at launch; this doc fully designs the anchors (starter trio +
> notable Wraiths + the Hollow legendary) and lists fill-ins. All designs are **100% original**.
>
> Stats use 5 axes (1–5 each, illustrative): **Vigor** (HP) · **Aether** (energy pool) · **Flow**
> (Aether regen) · **Power** (Strike damage) · **Spirit** (Resolve gain rate).

---

## The Starter Trio

Pick one to begin; the other two are bindable later. Classic collector hook, each teaching a different
*style* of flicking.

### 🔥 Ashling → Cindreaver → Pyrelich  (Ember)
*A guttering ember-fox spirit that never quite goes out.*
- **Identity:** aggressive tempo. Teaches *offense from defense* — punishes you into striking.
- **Base stats:** Vigor 2 · Aether 3 · Flow 3 · Power 4 · Spirit 4
- **Strikes:** *Cinder Snap* (Ember, fast 2-tick wind-up) · *Backdraft* (Ember, hits two consecutive ticks) · *Pyre Lash* (Ember, heavy, long wind-up).
- **Signature — "Everburn":** your Strikes apply **Sear** (opponent's next Ward costs double Aether). Reward: chain pressure until they can't afford to Ward.
- **Ascension (Pyrelich):** gains a Hollow-tinged second Strike *Soulscorch* that can't be Clean-Warded (Perfect or Graze only) — a budget skill-check finisher.

### 🌊 Drippet → Mournmaw → Sunkenlord  (Tide)
*A weeping lantern-jelly; later, a drowned leviathan-ghost.*
- **Identity:** attrition & control. Teaches *patience* — win by making them run dry.
- **Base stats:** Vigor 4 · Aether 4 · Flow 4 · Power 2 · Spirit 3
- **Strikes:** *Undertow* (Tide) · *Saltbite* (Tide, applies **Drown**) · *Deluge* (Tide, wide window but chip damage).
- **Signature — "Riptide":** when **you** land a Perfect Ward, drain a chunk of the opponent's Aether. Your perfect defense literally bankrupts their ability to defend. The purest "defense feeds offense" Wraith.
- **Ascension (Sunkenlord):** Riptide also refunds *you* Aether on Perfect — near-infinite Warding in skilled hands.

### 🌩 Sprite → Galewisp → Tempestrix  (Storm)
*A flickering wind-mote; later, a thunder-crowned storm-roc.*
- **Identity:** the trickster / execution check. Teaches *reads & feints*.
- **Base stats:** Vigor 2 · Aether 3 · Flow 4 · Power 3 · Spirit 5
- **Strikes:** *Gust* (Storm, fast) · *Forktongue* (Storm, **feint** — telegraphs one element, becomes Storm on the last beat) · *Thunderhead* (Storm, builds over 3 ticks).
- **Signature — "Doublestrike":** its Strikes telegraph as one hit but split into **two landing ticks** (forces *double-cover* reads). The highest-ceiling starter; the hardest to fight and to pilot.
- **Ascension (Tempestrix):** Doublestrike's two halves can be *different elements* — the ultimate mind-game engine and the face of our hook clip (§2 of the GDD).

## Notable Wraiths (mid-game anchors)

### ⛰ Cairnling → Bouldwight → Gravemount  (Stone)
*Walking cairn-stones; a mountain that remembers being a god.*
- **Role:** the wall / comeback pick. Vigor 5 · Aether 2 · Flow 2 · Power 4 · Spirit 2.
- **Signature — "Aegis":** once per battle, hold **one** Ward at *zero* Aether cost for several ticks — a panic button against a barrage, or a free pivot to offense. Teaches resource *banking*.
- **Ascension (Gravemount):** Aegis also reflects a portion of a negated Strike back as a counter.

### 🍄 Sporeling → Rotbloom → Mossgrave Hierophant  (Bloom)
*Fungal decay given will; a priest of rot.*
- **Role:** stall / disruption. Vigor 4 · Aether 4 · Flow 3 · Power 2 · Spirit 3.
- **Signature — "Wither":** passively applies **Sap** (opponent gains Resolve slower). Starves aggressive flickers of their offense — the anti-Pyrelich.
- **Ascension (Mossgrave Hierophant):** Wither also slowly heals you each tick all Wards are down — rewarding *immaculate* flicking with sustain.

### ❄ Rimeling → Frostbite → Glaciax  (Frost)
*Black-ice spirits; a glacier with a thousand frozen screams inside.*
- **Role:** the pressure pick. Vigor 3 · Aether 3 · Flow 3 · Power 4 · Spirit 3.
- **Signature — "Rimebrand":** Strikes apply **Rime** (briefly narrows the opponent's Perfect window — visible & counterable). Makes the opponent's flicking *measurably harder*. The skill-vs-skill tightener.
- **Ascension (Glaciax):** Rime can stack once, demanding truly clean play to survive.

## Hollow Legendary (endgame chase)

### 🕳 Voidmoth Mourne  (Hollow) ★
*The death-echo of the seventh thing — a moth of pure absence whose wings eat the dusk.*
- **Role:** the ultimate skill-check; the "did you actually learn the game?" boss & chase-Wraith.
- **Stats:** all rounded, but rules-breaking.
- **Mechanics that break the chart (on purpose, and visibly):**
  - **Type-null:** ignores the element wheel — you can't cheese it with matchup advantage; only timing saves you.
  - **Unmaking:** its Strikes apply the **Unmaking** curse — the struck Ward *decays*, so it can't be held, only **snap-flicked** (no lazy/Clean Wards allowed). Forces Perfect-or-pay.
  - **Cannot be fully negated:** even a Perfect Ward only *Grazes* its Strikes — you survive by minimizing, not denying. Reframes the whole game for the finale.
- **Bindable** only after the Story climax, via the hardest **Binding Rite** in the game. The trophy that says *I can flick*.

## Roster fill-ins (to reach ~24–30, design later)

Two-to-three more lines per element to round out team-building and the meta, e.g.:
- **Ember:** a magma-slug tank line; a will-o'-wisp glass-cannon line.
- **Tide:** a drowned-hound hunter line; a deep-angler ambush line.
- **Storm:** a static-eel chip line; a stormcrow harasser line.
- **Stone:** a barrow-knight bruiser line; a geode-beetle support line.
- **Bloom:** a thornvine controller line; a sporecloud evasion line.
- **Frost:** a frostwolf pressure line; a hoar-wraith stall line.
- **Dual-typed rares** (post-launch): e.g. Ember/Stone "Magmacairn," Tide/Frost "Brinewraith," etc.

### Design checklist for every new Wraith
- [ ] Does its signature **interact with the flick loop** (not just buff a stat)?
- [ ] Does it teach or test a **specific skill** (tempo / patience / reads / banking / clean play)?
- [ ] Is its silhouette **readable** at small size and in spectator HUD?
- [ ] Is it **fun to fight against**, not just to pilot?
- [ ] Original name & design that fits the **Vael naming bible** (see WORLD.md)?
