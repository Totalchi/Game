# WARDBOUND — Seasons & Live-Ops

> A worldwide hit isn't a launch, it's a *service*. Seasons give players a reason to return forever, a
> predictable cosmetic-revenue rhythm, and a steady drip of story, Wraiths, and chases — all without
> pay-to-win. This is the live-ops engine.

## 1. Season shape

- **Length:** ~**8–10 weeks** (long enough to enjoy, short enough to stay fresh).
- **Every season ships:**
  - 🎭 A **theme** + new **story chapter** (continuing the "held, not healed" thread from STORY.md).
  - 🐾 **New Wraiths** (incl. ≥1 Very Rare/Revenant chase + seasonal Aberrant variants).
  - 🗺️ Often a **new/awakened domain** or major zone event.
  - 🎟️ A new **Warden's Pact** (battle pass): free track + premium cosmetic track.
  - ⚔️ A **ranked split** (soft ladder reset, placement, season rewards = cosmetic, never power).
  - 🌑 A signature **world event** that drives the rare-spawn chase.

## 2. World events (the heartbeat of live-ops + the rarity engine)

These make the world feel *alive* and time-sensitive — the core "log in now" driver:

- **Knell Stutters** (recurring): the heartbeat falters world-wide; rare spawns surge, special Binding
  Rites open. Short, frequent, FOMO-light (they come back).
- **Eclipses** (seasonal marquee): a domain goes dark; an Echo or Hollowborn becomes briefly bindable;
  unique cosmetics drop. The big "be there" moment of a season.
- **Domain Storms** (rotating): elemental weather per domain unlocks conditional Wraiths + bonus rewards.
- **Community goals:** server-wide "tend the world" objectives (e.g. *collectively land N million Perfect
  Wards*) that unlock a global cosmetic + lore beat. Builds *shared* culture — powerful for retention.

## 3. The Warden's Pact (battle pass) — ethical & generous

- **Free track:** real, desirable cosmetics + premium-currency drops (goodwill + soft conversion).
- **Premium track:** cosmetics only — Wraith skins, **Ward-FX** (our prime real estate), sigils,
  Sanctuary décor, dyes, victory emotes. **No power, no rare creatures, ever.**
- **Earnable, not grindy:** completable with normal play; no "buy tiers to win." Optional tier-skips are
  cosmetic-only catch-up, never advantage.
- **Why it works here:** cosmetics are ~42% of in-game purchase revenue, and our most-watched action
  (the flick) is the most desirable cosmetic surface in the game. (See MONETIZATION.md.)

## 5. Cadence between seasons (so it never goes quiet)

- **Weekly:** rotating Trials, bounties, a Domain Storm, ranked decay nudge.
- **Bi-weekly:** a Knell Stutter event.
- **Mid-season patch:** balance pass + a "drop" (a Wraith or cosmetic set) to re-spike engagement.
- **Always-on:** Daily Ward, hunting, Sanctuary building, the full collection chase.

## 6. Live-ops principles (protect the soul & the skill)

1. **Never sell power or rares.** Cosmetics & access only. The day we break this, we lose the hardcore —
   and they're our marketing.
2. **FOMO gently.** Limited cosmetics rotate back eventually; we create *excitement*, not anxiety/regret.
3. **Balance is sacred & transparent.** Public patch notes, a PvP balance philosophy, fast hotfixes.
4. **Story keeps breathing.** Every season advances the world a beat — soul is a renewable resource.
5. **Listen loudly.** A skill+collector community is passionate; co-create with them (test servers,
   feedback loops). Their guides, clips, and fan art *are* the growth engine.
